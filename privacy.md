# Board Game Mate: Privacy Policy

*Effective 25 October 2026*

Board Game Mate ("the app") is a score keeper for board games, made by Markos Evlogimenos. This policy explains what
happens to your data. In short: **your data stays on your phone unless you connect BoardGameGeek, and then only goes
to BoardGameGeek.** There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer runs no servers that receive your data.

## Without BoardGameGeek (the default)
Everything you enter (plays, scores, players, games, locations, scorepads, settings) is stored only on your device.
The app does not send any of it anywhere.

## With BoardGameGeek (optional)
BoardGameGeek (BGG, boardgamegeek.com) is a third-party website. If you connect your BGG account, the app talks
directly to BGG, never through the developer:

- **Your plays.** Plays you upload, with their players, scores and notes, are saved to *your own* BGG account,
  automatically or when you choose (see Settings). The app also downloads your plays, and the plays of BGG users you
  add as players, from BGG.
- **Your password** is sent only to BoardGameGeek, to sign in, and is never stored. The app keeps only the sign-in
  session BGG returns, in your device's secure storage. Signing out deletes it.
- **Shared scorepads.** When you upload a play that uses one of your scorepads, the scorepad (its categories and
  formulas, the BGG game and your BGG username as its author) is published to the shared BGG account
  `bgmate_templates`, so other Board Game Mate users can use it. No players or scores are included.
- **Game information.** Game searches, game details, box art and player avatars are loaded from BGG.

What BGG does with data it receives is covered by BGG's own privacy policy
(https://boardgamegeek.com/privacy).

## Other things the app may contact
- **Google Play** shows its rating dialog if you tap *Rate Board Game Mate*.
- **Links** you tap (Discord, BGG pages) open in your browser or the matching app.
- **Export data** saves a file wherever you choose; the app sends it nowhere.

## Your choices and deleting data
- Use the app without BGG and nothing leaves your device.
- Turn off **Auto upload** and upload only the plays you pick.
- Delete plays in the app. If **Auto-delete remote plays** is on, they are deleted on BGG too; otherwise delete them
  on boardgamegeek.com.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on the device.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information for the developer.
